## Decisions I made
- **Who should feel at home:** a UK beginner who bought a chilli plant, killed it, and thinks they are "not a plant person".
- **Three words:** warm, homegrown, calm. Friendly, never childish.
- **Love and hate:** real phone photos from real windowsills and kitchens, plants as they are. Never styled cookbook shots, stock photos or cartoon chillies.
- **Colours:** the CoEngineers colours exactly, plus leaf green and chilli red for small details only.
- **One accent, one job:** orange marks the single main button on a page.
- **Two typefaces:** Space Grotesk and Inter. Small labels are set in Inter, so there is no third face.
- **Why the look suits my customer:** it is calm, warm and plain, with real windowsill photos and one orange, so someone who gave up after one dead plant feels welcome, not talked down to by experts or a cartoon. In my words: it feels like a proper club, not a shop.
- **Voice:** the six rules and the words we use and avoid, kept as drafted.
