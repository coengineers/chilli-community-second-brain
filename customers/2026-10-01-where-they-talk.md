### What hurts

- A poster with four chilli plants on a windowsill says the flowers wither and drop, and the tiny chillies fall off with them. UKClimbing forum, date not shown. https://www.ukclimbing.com/forums/off_belay/growing_chillies_-_what's_going_wrong-416892
- In a 2024 thread, one poster says a first batch of seedlings failed and died. Another says a bad year left them short of motivation. Overclockers UK forum. https://forums.overclockers.co.uk/threads/2024-chilli-growing-thread.18983595/

### What they do now

- Other posters in the UKClimbing thread suggest a bigger pot, misting, feeding with tomato food and pollinating by hand. The advice differs from reply to reply.
- One Overclockers poster tried again with different compost and sowing depth.
