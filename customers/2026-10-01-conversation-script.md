# Conversation script, 1 October 2026

All questions are about the past. None describes or sells the idea. Nobody has been contacted yet. Kept as drafted by Priya on 1 October 2026, the same as the 30 September script.

## Opening

"I'm finding out how people get on with growing chillies at home. I'm not selling anything. Could I ask about the last time you tried?"

## Questions

1. Tell me about the last time you bought or tried to grow a chilli plant. What happened?
   - What did you expect to happen, and what actually happened?
2. What did you do when it started to go wrong?
3. Who or what did you turn to for help, and what did you find?
4. What did you decide afterwards about trying again?
5. What did it cost you, in money, time, or how it made you feel about growing things?
6. What have you tried since, if anything?
7. What should I have asked you that I haven't?

## If they have never grown anything

Same opening and closing, then these instead of questions 1 to 6.

1. Tell me about the last time you thought about growing something to cook with. What happened?
2. What made you put it off, or stop before you started?
3. What have you seen or heard about growing chillies, and where did you see it?
4. Have you been given a plant or a kit before? What became of it?
5. What do you do now when you want fresh chillies or herbs for cooking?

## Closing

"Thank you. Do you know anyone else who bought a plant and lost it, who I could speak to?"

## What would change my mind

- Most of the three never tried again, or say a dead plant was no big deal. Then giving up after one dead plant is not the real problem.
- They got what they needed free, from videos or the seller, and were not stuck. Then a paid membership has less to answer.
- What stopped them was light, time or cost rather than know-how. Then a weekly plan will not fix it.
- None of the three fit my customer. Then the customer needs another look.
