## In their words (Testing)

### What hurts

- **Plants that died soon after arriving.** "First lot of plants didn't do well. Two died almost immediately". Bryan, review of a UK chilli farm on Trustpilot, 13 July 2026. He puts it down to the delivery being left outside in full sun, says his plants sit in shade and he checked soil moisture twice a day, and says one lost all its flowers but survived. https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.southdevonchillifarm.co.uk
- **A plant that could not be brought back.** Chageevan bought a 1 litre Carolina Reaper plant the year before and could not revive it, despite repeated tries. Same page, 30 March 2026. This is the closest match to someone who lost a plant and stopped. https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.southdevonchillifarm.co.uk
- **The more they fuss, the more it dies.** A reader says they have started to notice that the more attention they give a plant, the more likely it is to die. A reader comment on The Earthworm newsletter, poster not named, date not shown. General plant remark, not about chillies. Weakest source here. https://theearthworm.substack.com/p/the-criminally-underrated-deliciousness/comments

### What they do now

- **The seller says a dead plant is normal.** The farm replied to Chageevan that chillies are grown as annuals in the UK, that superhots in particular are expected to die and be started again each season, and that the customer should email for advice and watch its videos. Reply from the farm, 31 March 2026, same page.
- **A replacement fixes it.** MR bought a lot of very hot seedlings. Two yellowed, the farm sent replacements straight away, and MR was happy with the service. Same page, 2 May 2026.
