# Decisions

What is decided, what is being tested and what is still unknown about Chilli Community, each with a date and where it came from. Lines are never deleted or reworded: a change gets a dated line under "What changed", and an old line that no longer stands is marked "Replaced on <date>, see below".

Started on 23 September 2026, from the Business Summary of the same day.

## Decided

- Chilli Community is a chilli-only digital membership that helps beginners grow chillies, cook simple recipes, watch recipe demonstrations and share what they make. (20 Sep, confirmed 23 Sep. Founder direction and founder note, in sources)
- It is for beginner growers on a windowsill, a balcony, in a garden or in a greenhouse. (20 Sep, confirmed 23 Sep. Founder direction and founder note, in sources)
- The first pilot is paid, for 10 to 20 founding members, and digital only. (20 Sep, confirmed 23 Sep. Founder direction and founder note, in sources)
- No box, shop, seed swap, live sessions or events at first. (20 Sep. Founder direction, in sources)
- Chilli is run as a real business: real asks for money, a real paid pilot, real outreach and real publishing. (23 Sep. Decisions note, in business; Priya's answers, in sources)
- The chilli plants are bought. Cost, supplier, varieties and how many are not recorded. (On or before 23 Sep. Plants note, in business)
- The member app has four areas: Home, Recipes, Videos and Community. (23 Sep. Decisions note, in business; Priya's answers, in sources. Second source, 30 Sep: Priya's notes for the week and her paper sketch)
- The weekly challenge sits inside the community post, not in its own section. (23 Sep. Decisions note, in business; Priya's answers, in sources)
- The case study is Chilli's public reference, private until Priya shares it. (23 Sep. Decisions note, in business; Priya's answers, in sources)
- Chilli gets its own design system in week 4, as a variant of the CoEngineers one. (23 Sep. Decisions note, in business; Priya's answers, in sources) Replaced on 23 Sep, see below.
- The landing page is a Claude artifact until the Ship course. (23 Sep. Decisions note, in business; Priya's answers, in sources)
- Payments go through Gumroad at first. (23 Sep. Decisions note, in business; Priya's answers, in sources)
- Chilli uses the CoEngineers design system exactly, with its own wordmark (Priya, 23 Sep). Every CoEngineers value stays as it is; Chilli adds its own pod mark and two small extra colours for details. (23 Sep. Priya, in the W04 rerun; brand/DESIGN.md)

## Being tested (still open)

- Each week: one recipe, one short video, one challenge, one post. (20 Sep. Founder direction, in sources) Replaced on 23 Sep, see below.
- Each week: one recipe, one short video and one community post with the challenge inside. Settled when founding members use it week after week. (23 Sep. Decisions note, in business) Replaced on 30 Sep, see below.
- Each week: one recipe, one short video and one community post with a small job inside. Settled when founding members use it week after week. (30 Sep. Priya's notes for the week; Priya confirmed the small job is the challenge)
- Growing, cooking and sharing as the answer to too much passive screen time. It is Priya's view; nobody who fits has said so yet. Settled when people who fit say it in their own words (week 5 looks for this). (20 Sep. Founder direction and founder note, in sources)
- The first members are found through real outreach to Priya's own network. Who exactly is not picked yet. Settled when there is a list of who to ask first, each with a reason. (23 Sep. Priya's answers, in sources)

## Unknown (still open)

- Whether beginners want it enough to pay. No conversations are on file. Settled by what growers say (week 5), then by real asks. (20 Sep. Founder direction, in sources)
- The price and the founding-member terms. How members pay is now decided (Gumroad); the price and terms are not. Settled by a drafted price and terms (week 6), marked Testing until Priya confirms. (20 Sep. Founder direction, in sources)
- How the first 10 to 20 members will be found. (20 Sep. Founder direction, in sources) Moved to Being tested on 23 Sep, see below.
- The launch date. To be decided. (20 Sep. Founder direction, in sources)
- Seed packs: not decided either way, maybe later. To be decided. (23 Sep. Founder note, in sources)
