## Who it is for and their problem

Someone new to growing who bought a chilli plant at a garden centre, killed it, and thinks they are "not a plant person". Decided (Priya, in the lab; voice memo).

Their problem, as Priya sees it: they gave up after one dead plant, and spend too much time on screens. Testing.

## The product idea

Digital first. Each week, one recipe, one short video, one challenge and one post in the community (Testing). Friendly community (Decided). No shop, box or live sessions to begin with (Decided).

## Where things stand

### Decided
- Chilli-only membership for people new to growing.
- Digital first: no shop, no monthly box, no live sessions.
- The first customer is the person who killed a garden centre chilli plant.
- The community is friendly, not a forum of experts.
- Founding price 8 pounds a month, held while the member stays.
- Payments go through Gumroad at first.

### Testing
- The weekly set: recipe, short video, challenge, community post.
- Videos of two to three minutes.
- 10 to 20 founding members.
- Too much time on screens is the problem worth solving.
- If the first plant lives, people come back.

### Unknown
- Whether anyone would pay. A beginner who fits saying so in their own words, or paying, would answer it.
- How to find the first members, and who to ask first.
- The launch date.
- Whether a seed pack comes later.
- What a beginner does today after their plant dies.

## Three next steps

1. Talk to three people who bought a chilli plant and lost it. Ask about the last time, not about what they might buy.
2. Write down who you would ask first to be a founding member, and why each one fits.
3. Choose a launch date, or write down that you have not chosen one.
