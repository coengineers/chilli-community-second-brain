24 September 2026 to 23 December 2026. A living document: it keeps its name, and every change gets a dated line under Changes. Private.

## The goal

**At least 10 founding members paying, and week one open, by 23 December 2026.**

- Status: Testing. Claude's recommendation from your second brain, yours to confirm (Draft, Priya to confirm).
- Superseded on 22 October 2026 by `business/2026-10-01-90-day-plan.md`: the goal is now 20 paying members by the end of March 2027, with 10 paying founding members by 19 January 2027 as the 90-day mark. See Changes.

### Why this, why now

- The first pilot is paid, for 10 to 20 founding members, digital only (Decided, `business/DECISIONS.md`); 10 is the smallest pilot you have set.
- The offer, the price, the payment product, the member app, the ask scripts, the warm list and the outreach sequence all exist, and nobody has been asked (`offer/OFFER.md`). Paying members are the first real proof, and the one thing none of the work so far can give.

### Other goals weighed

- A decided price. Worth less on its own, and it comes from the asks, so it is month two's milestone inside this goal.
- An audience for the content. Worth something later; with no channel of your own open yet and nothing posted, it cannot pay for the pilot in 90 days. The content supports the asks instead.
