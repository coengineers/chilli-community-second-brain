# Chilli Community: content system

Last changed: 21 October 2026 (Week 24). Private. This is the plan you follow each week. Nothing in it is posted for you: you post everything yourself.

## Hours

- Three hours a week buys two posts, about an hour and a half each. Decided (Priya, 21 October 2026).
- Inside each hour and a half: taking the photo or video, making the piece, posting it in both places. Replying to comments is not counted.
- Post 1 always goes out. Post 2 can be light when a week runs short: a photo and one line, about half an hour. Decided (Priya, 21 October 2026, after Claude's suggestion).
- That one post fits an hour and a half is Testing. Nothing has been timed yet.

## Where you publish first

The Chilli playlist on the CoEngineers YouTube channel and Instagram, with the same moment on both. You make each moment once: a short video with the written version in its description, and the same photo or clip with a shorter caption on Instagram.

- Decided: Priya, 21 October 2026. Priya's own view: this is really two places, made from one piece.
- Testing: that these are the right places for beginners.
- Unknown: whether anyone sees it.
- Forums come later, if at all. Their rules ban links and pitching, so they could never carry the ask.

## What counts as a moment

Something that happened this week that a beginner could learn from or recognise, backed by a photo, a note or a few seconds of video. A topic on its own does not count. Decided (Priya, 21 October 2026).
