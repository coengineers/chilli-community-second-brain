A reply at any point ends that person's series.

- Answer as a person. A question gets a plain answer. Not now gets a date agreed together.
- Do not send their later messages.
- Write what they said, in their words, in your notes on them the same day.
- No reply after the third message is an answer. Leave it at least three months before trying again, and only with something new.
- Anyone who asks not to hear from Priya is never messaged again.
