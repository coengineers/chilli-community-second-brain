# Prototype test pack, 30 September 2026

The prototype is a clickable member app for Windowsill to Plate (offer version 3). Link: not shared. It is private to Priya's Claude account, so testers cannot open it until she publishes it with a public link (Publish, let anyone with the link view it, copy the link).

## What the prototype shows

Four areas: Home, Recipes, Videos, Community. The weekly path is on Home: three things to do (cook it, watch it, share it), each ticked off, with a progress bar.

- Week 1 recipe: simple chilli oil, 25 minutes, made with shop chillies.
- Week 1 video: making simple chilli oil, 3 minutes.
- Week 1 job: show your windowsill, with a photo post. The one real post is Priya's own windowsill photo. Other posts are tagged Example.
- Bonuses: the windowsill starter list and the rescue card.
- Every screen says "Prototype: nothing here is saved". There is no payment, sign-up or price step.

## Before the first test: phone check (Priya)

1. Open the prototype on your own phone, in the browser you would give a tester.
2. Do task 1 below yourself and time it. If it takes more than a few seconds, or you hesitate, change the prototype before Nadia tests it on 1 October.
3. Look at Home, Recipes, Videos and Community in turn. Check nothing is cut off and the tap targets are easy to hit.
4. Try posting a photo from the phone.

## Tester tasks (what to do, never how)

1. Find this week's job and mark it done. This is the main path.
2. Find this week's recipe and video, and say which you would do first.
3. Share a photo of your windowsill with the community.

## What to watch for

- Where they go first, and any pause of more than a few seconds.
- Whether they finish task 1 without help, and what they say while doing it.
- Whether the Example posts read as real members.
- Nadia, Tom and Jo have all heard the £8 ask. Note anything they say about price without being asked.
- Write down what they say in their own words.

## Two questions to close each test

- What did you expect to see that was not there?
- Would you come back next week, and what would bring you back?
