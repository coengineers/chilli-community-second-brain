# Prototype test pack, 1 October 2026

The prototype is a clickable member app for Windowsill to Plate. It is for learning what three people do with it, and it is thrown away once the tests have taught that. Nothing in it is saved.

- **What it tests:** can a first-time member find this week's job and mark it done without help?
- **Link:** https://claude.ai/artifact/B8E3PsRRHz5jxvpVeD7b8F
- **Link status:** private to Priya's Claude account. Testers cannot open it until Priya publishes it with a public link (Publish, let anyone with the link view it, copy the link). Publishing is Priya's step.

## What the prototype shows

Four areas: Home, Recipes, Videos, Community. Home holds the weekly path: three things to do (cook it, watch it, do the week's job), each ticked off, with a progress bar.

- Week 1 recipe: simple chilli oil, 25 minutes, made with shop chillies (draft).
- Week 1 video: making simple chilli oil, 3 minutes, pretend playback (draft).
- Week 1 job: show your windowsill, inside Priya's weekly post in Community.
- Community: Priya's own post, two posts tagged Example (not real members, no names, no reactions), a chilli reaction and a post-a-photo screen.
- Every screen says "Prototype: nothing here is saved". There is no price, payment, sign-up or start date.
- Photo: none yet. Priya's post carries a tile marked "Photo to come: Priya's own windowsill". Priya swaps in her own photo before the first test if she can.

## Before the first test: phone check (Priya)

Do this before Nadia tests it on Thu 1 Oct.

1. Publish the prototype with a public link and open that link in a private window on your own phone, in the browser you would give a tester.
2. Do tester task 1 yourself and time it. If it takes more than a few seconds, or you hesitate, change the prototype before Nadia sees it.
3. Look at Home, Recipes, Videos and Community in turn. Check nothing is cut off and the buttons are easy to hit.
4. Try posting a photo from the phone.

## Tester tasks (what to do, never how)

Read each out as written. Do not say what to press.

1. Find this week's job and mark it done. This is the main path.
2. Find this week's recipe and video, and say which you would do first.
3. Share a photo of your windowsill with the community.

## What to watch for

- Where they go first, and any pause of more than a few seconds.
- Whether they finish task 1 without help, and what they say while doing it.
- Whether the Example posts read as real members.
- Whether they ask when it starts. Lucy did, when she heard the deck. The start date is not set (Unknown).
- Nadia, Tom and Jo have all heard the £8 ask. Note anything they say about price without being asked.
- Write down what they say in their own words, within the hour.

## Two questions to close each test

- What do you think this is for?
- What got in your way?

Also ask: what did you expect to see that was not there?
