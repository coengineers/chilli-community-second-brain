Chilli Community. Version 1, updated 30 September 2026 (second Week 13 run). Plan status: Agreed by Priya, 30 September 2026.

Built from: the offer (version 4, Windowsill to Plate), the Gumroad product draft, the prototype and its test script, the customer evidence pack, the decisions list and the brand, all in the second brain. ## Who it is for

UK beginners who bought a chilli plant, killed it and think they are not a plant person, and beginners who never started, such as Megan R. Windowsill, balcony, garden and greenhouse are where they grow, not separate customers.

- Who first: the first 10 to 20 founding members, up to 20 places, paying through Gumroad. Nobody is named yet. Status: Decided. Source: Priya, 30 September 2026; the offer, "Limits and deadlines"; the decisions list (20 September, confirmed 23 September).
- The one thing a member must be able to do in week one: sign in and find this week's recipe, video and job in one place. Status: Decided. Source: Priya, 30 September 2026.

Not for version 1: anyone who wants seeds, plants or a box posted to them.

### Feature 1: This week

- **Week:** Week 15. **Why this place in the order:** It only shows what Priya has made and saves each member's own ticks: no harder signs. It is worth using on its own if the second feature slips.
- **Builds on:** The base app's sign-in and profile. Until feature 2, only test members set up during the build can get in. Claude adds the content as part of the build: week one's draft from the prototype (the chilli oil recipe) with a short test video, since no video is filmed yet, plus one earlier test week so Past weeks can be checked. Anything that is a test is labelled as a test.
- **Status:** Checked (built and checked on 23 September 2026 with the earlier scope; the starter list is dropped and the rescue card page is the only page under Home's "Yours from the start", to be rechecked in the build week).
- **Done test (Priya, 30 September 2026):** Start: signed in, on Home. Do: open This week. See: the recipe, the video and the job, with your ticks still there after you sign out and back in.
- **Done test, the rescue card (Priya, 30 September 2026, added at Claude's suggestion):** Start: signed in, on Home. Do: open the rescue card. See: one page on what your plant is telling you and the one thing to try this week.

**Stories**

1. As a founding member, I want this week's recipe, video and job in one place when I sign in, so that I know what to do this week instead of refereeing advice that disagrees.
2. As a founding member, I want to tick off the recipe, the video and the post, and find my ticks there next time, so that I can see how my week is going.
3. As a member who joins late or misses a week, I want every past week's recipe, video and post, so that I can catch up.
4. As a new member who killed a plant before, I want the rescue card from the start, so that I know what my plant is telling me and what to try.

**Checks**

### What version 1 must not do

- Take payment or hold card details. Gumroad takes the money; the stretch uses Stripe in test mode only.
- Send emails, reminders or notifications. The one exception is the sign-in link a member asks for.
- Invite, message or charge anyone on Priya's behalf. Priya sends every invitation herself.
- Let a member into Priya's area for adding and scheduling weeks.
- Let anyone in without a purchase, or twice for the same sale.
- Offer posts by members, comments, chat or direct messages.
- Promise a harvest or show results nobody has had. The offer promises the weekly recipe, video and job, and the first-month promise, nothing more.
- Rebuild sign-in, profiles, private files or the Claude connection. The base app already has them.
