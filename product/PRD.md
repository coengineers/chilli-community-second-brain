# Chilli Community app: product plan

Chilli Community. Version 1, updated 1 October 2026. Plan status: Agreed by Priya, 1 October 2026.

Built from: the offer (version 6, Windowsill to Plate), the Week 12 decision (Go, 1 October 2026), the app map, the prototype and its test script, the customer evidence pack, the decisions list and the brand, all in the second brain. The name Windowsill to Plate is Decided (Priya, 30 September 2026).

This is a living plan. The building weeks read its headings, so keep them as they are. Every claim carries a status (Decided, Testing or Unknown). Anything not yet known says "To be decided". The Plan page that shows this is in Priya's Claude account, called "Chilli Community plan" (https://claude.ai/artifact/TG2znceTzszMXw1tWUQVRP). It is private until Priya shares it. It has the Chilli logo and a drawing of a pot marked as a stand-in, because Priya has no windowsill photo of her own yet.

## Who it is for

UK beginners who bought a chilli plant, killed it and think they are not a plant person, and beginners who never started, such as Megan R. Windowsill, balcony, garden and greenhouse are where they grow, not separate customers.

- Who first: the first 10 to 20 founding members, up to 20 places, paying through Gumroad. The first three in, if they confirm by Wednesday 7 October 2026, would be Nadia, Megan and Jo, who said yes at £8 a month. Status: Decided. Source: Priya, 1 October 2026; the offer, "Limits and deadlines"; the Week 12 decision.
- The one thing a member must be able to do in week one: sign in and find this week's recipe, video and job in one place. Status: Decided. Source: Priya, 1 October 2026.

Not for version 1: anyone who wants seeds, plants or a box posted to them.

### Feature 1: This week

- **Week:** Week 15. **Why this place in the order:** It only shows what Priya has made and saves each member's own ticks: no harder signs. It is worth using on its own if the second feature slips.
- **Builds on:** The base app's sign-in and profile. Until payments and access (Week 17), only test members set up during the build can get in. Claude adds the content as part of the build: week one's draft from the prototype (the chilli oil recipe) with a short test video, since no video is filmed yet. Anything that is a test is labelled as a test.
- **Status:** Checked (built and checked on 23 September 2026 with the earlier scope; to be rechecked in the build week now the rescue card page and Past weeks are out).
- **Done test (Priya, 1 October 2026, unchanged):** Start: signed in, on Home. Do: open This week. See: the recipe, the video and the job, with your ticks still there after you sign out and back in.

**Stories**

1. As a founding member, I want this week's recipe, video and job in one place when I sign in, so that I know what to do this week instead of refereeing advice that disagrees.
2. As a founding member, I want to tick off the recipe, the video and the post, and find my ticks there next time, so that I can see how my week is going.

**Checks**

1. Start: Signed in as test member A, on Home. Do: Open This week. See: The week's recipe with its photo, ingredients, method and time; the short video; and the week's post with the job inside.
2. Start: Signed in as test member A, on This week. Do: Play the video and turn captions on. See: The video plays in the page, and the captions show in step with what is said.
3. Start: Signed in as test member A, on This week. Do: Tick the recipe, the video and the post, sign out, then sign back in. See: All three still ticked, and Home shows 3 of 3 done for this week.
4. Start: Signed in as test member A, on a phone-sized screen. Do: Open This week, read the method and tick the recipe. See: Everything fits the screen with no sideways scrolling, and the tick is easy to hit with a thumb.
5. Start: Signed in as test member A, on This week, with the connection switched off. Do: Tick the recipe. See: A plain message that the tick was not saved, and a Try again button. With the connection back, Try again saves it.
6. Start: Signed in as test member B, after member A has ticked all three. Do: Open This week. See: Nothing ticked. Each member sees only their own ticks.
7. Start: Signed out, on the test address. Do: Open the This week address directly. See: The sign-in page, and none of the week's content.

### Feature 2: The weekly post

- **Week:** Week 16. **Why this place in the order:** It is the first thing one member does that another member sees, so what stays private matters. It builds on This week and adds no money or timetable.
- **Builds on:** Feature 1's week and the base app's sign-in and private files. The app shrinks each photo, so nobody has to resize anything.
- **Status:** Checked in part: Tom posted the first photo and finished without help (Priya, 30 September 2026). The chilli count, the signed-out photo address, removing a post and the connection-drop message are still to try.

**Stories**

1. As a founding member, I want to share a photo of my plant on the week's post, so that other members can see how mine is doing.
2. As a founding member, I want to see other members' photos and add a chilli to one I like, so that it feels like a friendly place and not a forum of experts.
3. As a member, I want only signed-in members to see photos and names, so that what I share stays among members.

**Checks**

2. Start: Signed in as test member B. Do: Open the week's post. See: A's post, with name, photo, caption and chilli count.
3. Start: Signed in as test member B, on A's post. Do: Tap the chilli, reload, then tap again. See: 1, then 1, then 0.
4. Start: Signed in as test member A. Do: Choose a document instead of a photo. See: A plain message that only photos can be posted, and nothing posted.
5. Start: Signed in as test member A, on A's own post. Do: Remove it and confirm. See: The post and its photo are gone for everyone, and no remove option shows on anyone else's post.
6. Start: Signed out. Do: Open the exact address of a photo. See: The sign-in page, and no photo.
7. Start: Signed in as test member A, on a phone-sized screen. Do: Post a photo. See: Everything fits with no sideways scrolling.
8. Start: Signed in as test member A, with the connection switched off. Do: Post. See: A plain message that it did not post and a Try again button, with the photo and caption still there. With the connection back, Try again posts it once.

### Payments and access (Week 17, not a feature)

Payments stay a Gumroad link, and Priya adds founding members by hand. Week 17 makes who gets in, and how they pay, work end to end.

- **Week:** Week 17. **Why this place in the order:** Money and who gets in because of it, another company's service (Gumroad), things that happen on a timetable with nobody clicking, and a founder-only area members must never reach: every harder sign.
- **Builds on:** Features 1 and 2. Built and checked on 24 September 2026 against Gumroad's documented sale notification format with Gumroad's sample messages only. Connecting the live Gumroad product is a step for Priya.
- **Status:** Checked with sample messages. Nobody buys anything: Priya only checks her payment page.
- **Done test A, bought so in (Priya, 1 October 2026, unchanged):** Start: someone has bought on Gumroad. Do: sign in with the same email. See: this week straight away.
- **Done test B, refunded so out (Priya, 1 October 2026, changed at Claude's suggestion):** Start: two members got in by buying, and one of them has been refunded in Gumroad. Do: each of them reloads the app. See: the refunded member sees "membership not active" and none of the week's content, and the other member still sees this week.

**Stories**

1. As someone who has just bought the founding membership on Gumroad, I want to sign in with the same email and be straight in, so that I can start this week without waiting for Priya.
2. As Priya, I want to add next week's recipe, video and post and choose when it opens, so that the week is ready before members arrive.
3. As a member, I want the new week to open on its own, so that I find it without asking.
4. As Priya, I want only people who have paid, or whom I added by hand, to get in, and anyone refunded to lose access, so that the 20 founding places stay fair.

**Checks**

1. Start: A sample sale notification in Gumroad's documented format arrives. Do: Sign in with the email it was sent for. See: This week, straight away, with no other step.
2. Start: Signed out, with an email that has no purchase. Do: Sign in with that email. See: A plain page saying the membership is not active yet and how to join, and none of the week's content.
3. Start: The same sample sale notification arrives a second time. Do: Open the members list in Priya's area. See: One member for that email, not two.
4. Start: Members C and D both got in by a sample sale. Do: Send a sample refund notification for C's sale, then both reload the app. See: C sees the membership-not-active page and none of the week's content, and D still sees this week.
5. Start: A notification that did not come from Chilli's Gumroad product (a wrong private address or another product). Do: Send it. See: Nobody is let in, and the members list does not change.
6. Start: Signed in as the founder, in Priya's area. Do: Add next week's recipe (photo, ingredients, method, time), video with captions and post, set to open in five minutes. See: It shows as Scheduled with its opening time, and members still see this week.
7. Start: The opening time has passed. Do: Member A reloads Home. See: The new week as This week, with last week's content no longer shown there.
8. Start: Signed in as member A. Do: Open Priya's area address directly. See: A plain page saying it is not for members, and none of next week's content.
9. Start: Signed in as the founder on a phone-sized screen. Do: Add a week and schedule it. See: Every field fits with no sideways scrolling, and it saves.
10. Start: Signed in as the founder, adding a week. Do: Save with no video and no opening time. See: A plain message naming what is missing, and nothing already typed is lost.

### What version 1 must not do

- Take payment or hold card details. Gumroad takes the money; the stretch uses Stripe in test mode only.
- Send emails, reminders or notifications. The one exception is the sign-in link a member asks for.
- Invite, message or charge anyone on Priya's behalf. Priya sends every invitation herself.
- Let a member into Priya's area for adding and scheduling weeks.
- Let anyone in without a purchase, or twice for the same sale.
- Offer comments, chat or direct messages. A member can only share a photo on the week's post.
- Promise a harvest or show results nobody has had. The offer promises the weekly recipe, video and job, and the first-month promise, nothing more.
- Rebuild sign-in, profiles, private files or the Claude connection. The base app already has them.
