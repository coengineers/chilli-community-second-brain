# Plan: This week (first piece of the Chilli Community app)

1 October 2026. Agreed by Priya, 1 October 2026. Built from the product plan (version 1, updated 1 October 2026), the app map and the Week 14 notes. This replaces the plan of 30 September 2026.

## The piece in one line
A signed-in member opens This week and finds the recipe, the video and the job in one place, and ticks each one off. Home opens on this week's job.

Who uses it: founding members, up to 20, UK beginners. Until the members piece, only test members set up during the build can get in.

## Done test (Priya, 1 October 2026, confirmed before building)
- Start: signed in, on Home.
- Do: open This week.
- See: the recipe, the video and the job, with your ticks still there after you sign out and back in. It works on a phone.

## Steps, in build order
1. **The recipe.** Photo, ingredients, method and time, from a practice week labelled Practice.
2. **The video.** A short test video plays on the page with captions, labelled as a test until Priya films one.
3. **The job.** The week's post with the job inside. Members read it and do not post to it.
4. **The ticks.** A member ticks the recipe, the video and the job. The ticks are saved for that member, and Home shows "3 of 3 done".
5. **Phone and trouble.** The page fits a phone with no sideways scrolling. If a tick cannot be saved, the member sees a plain message and a Try again button.

## Decisions made in this plan
- Home opens on this week's job (from Priya's Week 14 phone note, Priya, 1 October 2026).
- Videos are kept on an unlisted video site, not the private files (Priya, 1 October 2026). Anyone with the exact link could watch a video. Which site: To be decided.

## What stays private
- Someone who is not signed in sees the sign-in page and none of the week's content.
- Each member sees only their own ticks.
- Until the members piece (Week 16), only test members can get in.

## When something goes wrong
If a tick cannot be saved, the member sees a plain message that it was not saved and a Try again button. With the connection back, Try again saves it.

## What must not change
Sign-in, a member's name, the Chilli look, and the button text and wordmark fixes made after Week 14. Past weeks and Show your windowsill stay out of members' sight.

## By hand for now
Priya writes each recipe and job and films each video. Until then the practice week is labelled Practice and the video is labelled as a test.

## Not in this piece
Members posting, notifications, emails from the app, payment, and the way in for paying members (Weeks 16 and 17).
