## The steps, in build order

1. **This week page.** One practice week: a chilli oil recipe with photo, ingredients, method and time; a short test video labelled as a test, with captions; and the week's job.
2. **Ticks that stay.** The member ticks the recipe, the video and the job. The ticks are saved for them and are still there after signing out and back in.
3. **Home opens on this week's job.** The job is at the top, the recipe and video just below it, and "3 of 3 done" once all are ticked. The Week 14 Home words stay.
4. **Past weeks.** One earlier practice week, so a member can open it and see its recipe, video and job with their own ticks.
5. **Yours from the start.** The windowsill starter list and the rescue card, each on its own page from Home. Labelled as practice until Priya writes them.

## What stays private

- Each member sees only their own ticks.
- A signed-out visitor gets the sign-in page and none of the week's content, even from the This week address.
- The video is the one exception: anyone holding its exact link could watch it. Priya accepted this.

## When something goes wrong

If the connection drops while a member ticks something, they see a plain message that the tick was not saved and a Try again button. With the connection back, Try again saves it.
