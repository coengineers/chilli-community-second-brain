## If they hesitate

### "I can get it all free on YouTube."
- **May mean:** "I'm not sure this is different from what I can already find."
- **Honest answer:** "The free stuff is good, and it disagrees with itself: sow in January, sow in April. This is one plan for a UK windowsill, a recipe for what you grow, and other beginners on the same week. And your first month is covered by the promise."

### "Isn't it cheaper to just buy chillies?" (Sam's answer was close to this)
- **May mean:** "Will I spend money on kit and get nothing back?"
- **Honest answer:** "There's nothing else to buy: no propagator, no grow light. £8 a month is less than the cheapest grow kit I found, £13.35, and the recipes are worth cooking with shop chillies anyway."

### "What if I kill it?" (Nadia and Jo both asked this)
- **May mean:** "I've failed before, and I don't want to fail again, paying for it."
- **Honest answer:** "I can't promise you a harvest, and I won't. What I can promise: a recipe, a video and a job every week, other beginners alongside you, and your first month back if it hasn't helped."

### "It's the wrong time of year."
- **May mean:** "Why pay now when nothing can grow until spring?"
- **Honest answer:** "You start by cooking. The weeks before sowing are recipes and getting ready. Sowing starts in January, or you keep a plant going over winter, or start from a plant in spring."

### "I usually buy a plant."
- **May mean:** "I don't want to fuss with seeds."
- **Honest answer:** "Good, the plan works from a bought plant too. Getting the plant is the easy part; keeping it going, getting chillies and cooking with them is what the weeks are for."

### "Money is tight." (Dee's reason)
- **May mean:** "I'd like it, but not this month."
- **Honest answer:** "That makes sense. Nobody pays until it opens, and I'll tell you the date first. The founding price is already the price for going first, so I won't lower it."

### "Can't I just have it free, as a friend?"
- **Honest answer:** "That's kind, but I'd rather you paid the founding price: people paying is how I find out whether this is worth doing. If the first month hasn't helped, you get it back."

### "When does it start?"
- **Honest answer:** "The date isn't set yet. Nobody pays until it's open, and you'll hear the date from me first."

Never cut the price to win a yes.

## Taking a no well

1. **Thank them.** "Thanks for being straight with me. That helps."
2. **Ask one question:** "What would have made it a yes?"
3. **Write down their exact words** in the tracker the same day. Their phrasing, not your summary.
4. **Do not chase.** One no is an answer.
5. **"Not now" gets a date.** "When would be a better time to ask again?" Agree it together and write it down.

A no this week is the most useful thing you will hear. Ten noes that all mention time say something different from ten that all mention price.
