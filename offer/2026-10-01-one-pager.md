## The words on the page

- For: UK beginners who killed a chilli plant or never started, on a windowsill, balcony, in a garden or greenhouse.
- Headline: "Killed a chilli plant? Try again with one clear job a week, and your first month back if it doesn't help."
- The problem: everyone gives different advice, one plant dies, and you decide you are no good with plants.
- What changes: one plan, one voice, set for an ordinary UK windowsill.
- What you get, five things:
  1. One plan, one voice: the same steps every week, so there is nothing to choose between.
  2. The week's job: one small job for your plant, not a big guide.
  3. Start where you are: the first weeks begin with cooking, not sowing, so autumn is not a reason to wait.
  4. A recipe and a short video, every week: works with shop chillies from week one, so it feels worth it before anything grows.
  5. The first-month promise.
- The first-month promise: "Kill your chilli plant? Your first month is back. Cook the recipe, watch the video and do the week's job. If it hasn't helped by the end of week four, tell Priya and she refunds your first month in full."
- Price: founding price, £8 a month, held at £8 for as long as the member stays. Up to 20 founding places. Online only, paid through Gumroad. Start date to be announced.
