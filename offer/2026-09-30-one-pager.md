## The words on the page

- For: beginners in the UK who want to grow chillies on a windowsill, a balcony, in the garden or a greenhouse.
- Headline: "Bought a chilli plant and it died? Try again, with a recipe, a video and a small job every week."
- The problem: seeds that never come up; flowers that drop before they turn into chillies; one dead plant and you decide you are not a plant person.
- What changes: one plan for a UK windowsill, with other beginners on the same week; you start cooking in week one, with shop chillies, while your own plant grows.
- What you get: a recipe every week; a short video; one small job; show your windowsill; two pages to start (the windowsill starter list and the rescue card); every past week stays, online only.
- A question people ask: "Is it all right if I have no south-facing window?" Answer on the page: every weekly job is written for an ordinary UK windowsill, with a pot and what you already have; if yours is a poor spot, tell Priya and she will say what to try.
- Founding price: £8 a month, held at £8 for as long as you stay, up to 20 founding places, stop any time, nobody pays until week one opens and Priya tells you the date first.
- First-month promise: cook the recipe, watch the video and do the week's job for four weeks; if it has not helped, tell Priya before the four weeks are up and she refunds your first month in full.
- Next step: "Reserve a founding place: reply to the message this came with."
