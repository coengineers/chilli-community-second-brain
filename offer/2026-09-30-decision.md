## Why Go

- The go line is met.
- There is no Pivot pattern. There is one no, and one person cannot make a pattern. The two not-nows give different reasons (timing and money), and neither is about the offer.
- It is an early signal, not proof. Nobody has paid, and nobody pays until week one opens. The launch date is Unknown.

## The change to the offer

One change, to the words of the first-month promise. The condition is unchanged.

- **Before:** "For your first four weeks, cook the recipe, watch the video and do the week's job. If you have done that and it has not helped, tell Priya before the four weeks are up and she refunds your first month in full."
- **After:** "Kill your chilli plant? Your first month is back. Cook the recipe, watch the video and do the week's job. If it hasn't helped by the end of week four, tell Priya and she refunds your first month in full."
- **Why:** Nadia and Jo both asked "what if I kill it?" before saying yes. The refund was part of how they decided, and the old wording did not answer that question in their words. Status: Testing.

Left alone:

- **Price, £8 a month.** Dee's reason was money this month, and the offer says never to cut the price to win a sale.
- **Sam's no.** "I'd rather just buy chillies" is already answered in the offer under "I can get it free" and "I'll just buy a plant". One no is not enough to change the offer.

## What would change the call

- **To Retest:** fewer than three of the yes people pay or confirm when week one opens.
- **To Pivot:** the next round of asks brings several noes with the same reason.
- **To Stop:** none of the yes people go through when joining opens.

**Confirm-by date: 7 October 2026.** Nadia, Megan and Jo are asked to confirm their place by then. The Retest and Stop lines are checked against that day, because the launch date is not set yet.
