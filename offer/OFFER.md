## The offer in one line

Try again with one clear thing to do each week, and your first month back if you kill it. A chilli-only membership for UK beginners: one recipe you can cook with shop chillies, a short video of it cooked, and one small job for your plant, every week.

- Status: Testing. Source: `customers/2026-09-30-customer-conversations.md`; the chilli-only digital membership itself is Decided (`business/DECISIONS.md`, 20 Sep 2026, confirmed 23 Sep).

## Who it is for

UK beginners who tried to grow chillies and gave up, or who never started.

- The core customer is Decided: someone new to growing who bought a chilli plant, killed it and thinks they are "not a plant person" (Priya, 30 Sep 2026, `customers/2026-09-30-customer-and-problem.md`).
- Beginners who never started, such as Megan R., are Testing. Why she has never started is Unknown.
- Windowsill, balcony, garden and greenhouse are where they grow, not separate customers (Decided).

Not for: anyone who wants seeds, plants or a box posted to them. The first version is digital only (Decided, `business/DECISIONS.md`).

## Their problem, in their words

In their words:

> "Everyone says something different about watering, so I stopped." Nadia S.

> "I read three websites and they disagreed." Tom H.

> "Nothing grows in autumn anyway, so what would I even do now?" Nadia S.

> "What if I kill it?" Megan R., who has never grown anything. Her first question when Priya described the idea.

Priya's notes of what they said (not their exact words): Tom's flowers dropped and he did not know why. Nadia tried twice on a windowsill and nothing came up. Nadia wanted one clear thing to do each week, not a big guide. Tom would want to cook something early on, so it feels worth it before anything grows.

Status: Testing. All practice conversations, simulated for the course. Source: `customers/2026-09-30-customer-conversations.md`.

## What they get

Each part answers something a customer said gets in the way.

| What gets in the way | What they get | How it is delivered | Who said it | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Advice that disagrees | **One plan, one voice.** The same steps every week, set for a UK windowsill, so there is nothing to choose between. | Written, in the Recipes and Home areas of the member app, every week | Nadia S., Tom H. (their words) | Testing |
| No idea why it went wrong | **The rescue card.** One page on what your plant is telling you, such as nothing coming up or flowers dropping, and the one thing to try this week. | One page in the member app | Tom H., Nadia S. (Priya's notes) | Testing |

## Guarantee

**The first-month promise.** Kill your chilli plant? Your first month is back. Cook the recipe, watch the video and do the week's job. If it hasn't helped by the end of week four, tell Priya and she refunds your first month in full.

- Type: conditional refund. It promises only what Priya controls (the weekly recipe, video and job, and the refund), never a harvest.
- Status: wording confirmed by Priya, 30 Sep 2026. Whether it helps anyone is Testing. Source: Megan R. ("What if I kill it?").

## Price

**Status: Testing.** Nobody has been asked about a price, so nobody has said yes or no to it.

**£8 a month, the founding price, held for as long as the member stays, paid through Gumroad.** Priya chose £8 on 24 Sep 2026 and confirmed it in the Week 6 lab, 30 Sep 2026.

Why £8, as Priya confirmed:
- It sits under the cheapest grow kit in her research: £13.35 for a four-variety kit (desk research, seen 23 Sep 2026).
- It is under half the £17.99 monthly seed box, which posts physical things this does not (desk research, seen 23 Sep 2026).
- There is nothing else to buy.

Version 3's other options, £40 for six months and £12 a month for later rounds, are not carried forward.
