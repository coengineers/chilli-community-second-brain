## The offer in one line

Try again with one clear thing to do each week, and your first month back if it has not helped. A chilli-only membership for UK beginners: one recipe you can cook with shop chillies, a short video of it cooked, and one small job for your plant, every week.

- Status: Testing. Source: `customers/2026-10-01-customer-conversations.md`; the chilli-only digital membership itself is Decided (`business/DECISIONS.md`, 20 Sep 2026, confirmed 23 Sep).

## Who it is for

UK beginners who tried to grow chillies and gave up, or who never started.

- The core customer is Decided: someone new to growing who bought a chilli plant, killed it and thinks they are "not a plant person" (Priya, 30 Sep 2026, `customers/2026-09-30-customer-and-problem.md`).
- Beginners who never started, such as Megan R., are Testing. Why she has never started is Unknown.
- Windowsill, balcony, garden and greenhouse are where they grow, not separate customers (Decided).

Not for: anyone who wants seeds, plants or a box posted to them. The first version is digital only (Decided, `business/DECISIONS.md`).

## What they get

Each part answers something a customer said gets in the way.

| What gets in the way | What they get | How it is delivered | Who said it | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|

The weekly set is one recipe, one short video and one community post with a small job inside (Testing, `business/2026-09-30-business-snapshot.md`). The four areas of the member app are Home, Recipes, Videos and Community (Decided, `business/DECISIONS.md`). The community is where the set lives. No customer in the three conversations named it, so it is not a separate part of the offer (Priya's choice, 1 Oct 2026).

Cut, because nobody in the three conversations asked for it: the rescue card (nobody said not knowing why it went wrong gets in the way), live sessions, one-to-one help, a box of seeds or plants, and a long growing guide. No box, shop, seed swap, live sessions or events at first is also Decided (`business/DECISIONS.md`).

## Guarantee

**The first-month promise.** Kill your chilli plant? Your first month is back. Cook the recipe, watch the video and do the week's job. If it hasn't helped by the end of week four, tell Priya and she refunds your first month in full.

- Type: conditional refund. It promises only what Priya controls (the weekly recipe, video and job, and the refund), never a harvest.
- Status: wording confirmed by Priya, 1 Oct 2026. Whether it helps anyone is Testing. Source: Megan R. ("What if I kill it?"); also asked by Jo (Priya, 1 Oct 2026).

## Price

**Status: Testing.** Three of eight people asked have said yes at this price. Nobody has paid.

**£8 a month, the founding price, held for as long as the member stays, paid through Gumroad.** Priya chose £8 on 24 Sep 2026 and confirmed it again in the Week 6 lab, 1 Oct 2026, over £12 a month and £40 for six months upfront. Kept in the Week 12 decision, 1 Oct 2026.

Why £8, as Priya confirmed:
- It sits under the cheapest grow kit in her research: £13.35 for a four-variety kit (desk research, seen 23 Sep 2026, not checked again).
- It is under half the £17.99 monthly seed box, which posts physical things this does not (desk research, seen 23 Sep 2026, not checked again).
- There is nothing else to buy.

Not chosen: £12 a month (beginners who have just given up are the least likely to commit before they trust it) and £40 for six months upfront (it asks someone whose first question was "What if I kill it?" to commit for six months).
