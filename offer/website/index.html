Questions

What people ask before joining

Can't I get all this free on YouTube?

The free advice is real, and it disagrees with itself: January or April? Windowsill to Plate gives you one plan for a UK windowsill, a recipe for what you grow, and people on the same week as you. Your first month is covered by the promise.

Isn't it cheaper to just buy chillies?

There's nothing else to buy: no propagator, no grow light. The founding price is less each month than the cheapest grow kit we found (£13.35), and the recipes are worth cooking with shop chillies too.

Isn't it the wrong time of year to start?

You start by cooking, whatever the month. The weeks before sowing are recipes and getting ready. Sowing starts in January, or keep a plant going over winter, or start from a plant in spring.

I usually just buy a plant. Is this still for me?

Yes. The plan works from a bought plant too. Getting a plant is the easy part; keeping it going, getting chillies and cooking with them is what the weeks are for.

Will this actually work for me?

Nobody can promise a harvest. What is promised: a recipe, a video and a job every week, other beginners alongside you, and your first month back if it hasn't helped.
