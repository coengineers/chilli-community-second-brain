Imagery

Real phone photos in real homes. These three are illustrative stand-ins until Priya's own photos replace them.

Use
  • Phone photos in real homes: kitchen windowsills, balconies, back gardens, a greenhouse bench
  • The real kit: black plastic pots, canes and twine, compost on newspaper, a watering can
  • Plants as they are, leggy or dropping a leaf, photographed from the same spot week by week
  • Hands at work: sowing, tying in, picking, chopping
  • Food as it comes out of the pan, on the plate it was served on, in daylight
Never
  • Styled cookbook shots, props and studio light
  • Stock photos of any kind
  • Doodle cards, cartoon chillies or anything childish
  • Flames, fire and heat-challenge clichés
  • A generated picture standing in for a member, a harvest or a result

Voice

Plain, warm British English, like a friend at the windowsill. Drafted from how Priya writes; Priya to confirm.

How we sound
  • Plain, warm British English, the way you would say it at the windowsill: short sentences, plain words.
  • Every tip is something a beginner can do this week, with what they already have.
  • Honest when things go wrong: leggy plants, dropped flowers, a batch that failed.
  • Warm and a bit dry. Never gushing, never cheerleading.
  • Real over polished: say what happened and show the real photo.
  • Never promise results. Say what is decided, what is being tested and what is not known yet.
