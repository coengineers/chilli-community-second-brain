# Week 5: Talk to your customers

Example. Use only for practice.

## What happened

Research in online gardening forum threads and groups found beginners asking whether a windowsill could grow chillies at all (no parenting forums). The script asked about the last time they tried. Nadia, Tom and Megan talked. Nadia had tried twice and nothing came up; Tom's bought plant flowered and then dropped its flowers; Megan had never tried.

## People

Priya · Nadia S., Tom H., Megan R.

## Example dialogue

- Nadia S. · “Everyone says something different about watering, so I stopped.”
- Nadia S. · “Nothing grows in autumn anyway, so what would I even do now?”
- Tom H. · “I read three websites and they disagreed.”
- Megan R. · “What if I kill it?”

Two of three said the advice disagreed. That became the first part of her offer.
