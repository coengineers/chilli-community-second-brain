# Week 25: Produce and publish

Example. Use only for practice.

## What happened

One moment became a short video, an Instagram post and a card.

## People

Priya · Aisha, Someone Priya does not know, Someone from Aisha’s village group

## Example dialogue

- Aisha · “Still the crispy one. Don’t judge me.”
  a comment
- Someone Priya does not know · “Supermarket basil and a very dead mint. Does that count?”
  a comment
- Someone from Aisha’s village group · “Aisha put your thing in our group. Is there still a place?”
  a message

- Result · Priya published "The plants I bought in September" on Instagram, the first place she chose, on Wednesday 28 October. By Saturday 31 October it had 19 likes, four comments and one message.

- What changed · The windowsill question got the answers, so it stays the last line of every post. Priya answered each comment herself and sent the person who asked the landing page.
