# Week 23: Start conversations

Example. Use only for practice.

## What happened

Eight people, three messages each over ten days. Two replies in the first week.

## People

Priya · Aisha, Hannah, Ravi, Beth, Chloe, Owen, Grace, Luca

## Example dialogue

- Aisha · “Most of us, me included. Send me two lines and I’ll put them in the group tonight. Nothing that reads like an advert or they’ll mute me.”
  Thursday 22 October, to message 1 (the same reply as Week 22)
- Ravi · “Three came up and now they’re all leaning at the window like they want to get out. Is that bad?”
  Friday 23 October, to message 1 ("has the chilli kit come up yet?")

- What changed · Both replies ended those two series. The other six carried on. Priya answered each as a person: Aisha got the two lines that afternoon, and Ravi got "turn the pot every day and move it to your brightest window". She asked Ravi about a founding place in her own words on Tuesday 27 October, the day she had set.
